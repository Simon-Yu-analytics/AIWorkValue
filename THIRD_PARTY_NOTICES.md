# Attribution and distribution boundaries

## Original experiment and replication materials

Shakked Noy and Whitney Zhang (2023). *Experimental evidence on the productivity effects of generative artificial intelligence*. Science 381, 187–192. DOI: https://doi.org/10.1126/science.adh2586.

Author-hosted replication package: https://osf.io/xd7qw/ (DOI: 10.17605/OSF.IO/XD7QW). Consult its original ReadMe and authors' instructions when downloading the source files.

AIWorkValue did not recruit the participants, run the experiment, collect the original data or author the source Stata scripts. Its contribution is independent Python sample reconstruction, estimator comparison, SQL summaries, aggregate visualizations and a hypothetical commercial extension. The main estimates are not presented as an exact replication of every published specification.

The OSF node declared no license in its metadata when inspected for this project. Public availability is not treated as a grant of redistribution rights. The raw files, author scripts, author ReadMe and participant-level derivatives are therefore excluded from the distributable archive and ignored by Git. They are downloaded separately by the user using the URLs and hashes in `data/source-manifest.json`.

The project's MIT license covers the original project code and documentation. It does not relicense source research materials, third-party dependencies or the original dataset. Aggregate statistical outputs in this package are generated by the project's analysis; they do not include participant records or written responses.

## Software dependencies

Python dependencies are listed in `requirements.txt` and installed separately. They are not vendored in this archive and retain their respective licenses.

## Interpretation

The source experiment studied short professional-writing tasks in early 2023. This package does not establish present-day product performance, full employee productivity, writing factual accuracy, a company deployment or realized cash savings. Commercial inputs are explicit hypothetical assumptions, not observed company measurements or current vendor price quotes.
